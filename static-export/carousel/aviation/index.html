Aviation Training Simulators for Airports, Airlines and Ground Handlers

Tecknotrove designs and manufactures training simulators and VR training solutions for the aviation industry. Our simulators are built for airports, airlines, ground handlers, aviation training institutes and MRO training institutes, and they cover the equipment and vehicles that operate on the airside: aircraft pushback and towing, cargo loaders, baggage tractors, catering trucks, passenger buses, fire trucks and airside light vehicles.

Every system is designed and built at our manufacturing facility in Gujarat, India, and supplied worldwide through authorised distribution and integration partners in India, the Middle East, Africa, Europe and Southeast Asia. Since 2002, Tecknotrove has delivered more than 1,000 simulation projects across 28 countries.

GSE Simulators

Ground support equipment simulators replicate the cab, controls and dynamics of the equipment your operators use during aircraft turnaround. Operators learn equipment handling, aircraft-specific procedures and incident response without risk to aircraft, people or GSE.

  • Aircraft Pushback & Towing SimulatorTowbar and towbarless pushback training on narrow and wide-body aircraft.
  • Cargo Loader & High Loader SimulatorMain deck and lower deck loading on Airbus and Boeing aircraft types.
  • Baggage Tractor SimulatorSafe towing of baggage carts across aprons and GSE roads.
  • Hi-Lift Catering Truck SimulatorLift, dock and service procedures at the aircraft door.
  • Passenger Boarding Bridge Simulator[confirm offered] bridge positioning and docking to the aircraft.

Airside Driving Simulators

Airside driving simulators train, up-skill and objectively test everyone who drives on the airfield. Drivers practise airside rules, markings, route familiarisation and emergency response in a virtual replica of the airport, in day, night and low-visibility conditions.

  • Airside Driving SimulatorLight vehicle training on SUV, sedan and follow-me vehicles.
  • Airport Passenger Bus SimulatorApron bus driving between terminals and remote stands.
  • ARFF / Fire Truck SimulatorEmergency response driving for airport rescue and firefighting crews.
  • Fuel Tanker SimulatorRefuelling vehicle driving and positioning on the apron.
  • Ramp Safety Simulator (VR)Hazard awareness and FOD identification on the ramp.

Aircraft Maintenance Training Solutions

VR and AR training modules prepare line maintenance engineers, mechanics and ground staff to inspect and service aircraft in a fully immersive environment. Modules are available for narrow and wide-body aircraft from Airbus and Boeing.

  • AR/VR Maintenance Manual DigitisationExisting maintenance manuals converted into immersive, hands-on modules.

Simulation Technologies

Simulation Technologies

Fidelity range

low fidelity portable simulators with a built-in instructor station, medium fidelity simulators with a separate instructor station and motion option, and high fidelity simulators with advanced instructor station, motion system and 180-degree curved projection.

VR and desktop versions

VR modules and desktop simulators for pre-employment screening, familiarisation and safety training at lower cost.

Virtual airside environment

GSE roads, aprons, markings and signage designed to ICAO standards, with remote bays, aerobridges, narrow and wide-body aircraft, terminals, taxiways and runways.

Instructor software and testing

trainee database, real-time performance evaluation, fault injection, and record and replay of every session.

Group training

a team play plugin that puts several operators in the same airside environment.

Motion and display

3DOF and 6DOF electric motion platforms; single 32-inch, triple 32-inch or 180-degree curved projection displays.

Convertible kits

multiple equipment types run on one simulator base unit.
