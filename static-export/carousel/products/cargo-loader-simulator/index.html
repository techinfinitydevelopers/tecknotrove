Key Features

What is in the Cargo Loader Simulator

Instructor Station & Software

The TechnoSIM instructor station lets the instructor set up scenarios, monitor progress and generate trainee feedback reports.

  • Trainee registration
  • Equipment selection
  • Real-time weather selection
  • Training and route selection
  • Real-time fault injection
  • Record and replay

Operator Station

A cab-style operator station with steering, instrument and control panel and pedals, replicating the dynamics and operations of the actual high loader.

  • Controls laid out as on the real equipment
  • Compatible with a variety of equipment models
  • Realistic operation and dynamics

Testing & Evaluation

The Performance Analysis System gives a detailed, consistent and scientific report on every session.

  • Graphical session reports
  • Major and minor offences highlighted
  • Operator testing and certification

Group Training

Tecknotrove Team Play lets multiple airside operators train together in the same environment.

  • Wing walkers, break rider, ATC, headset man, ramp manager
  • Collective training minimises errors and incidents
  • Improves aircraft turnaround time

TMS

Tecknotrove Training Management System® is a cloud-based system to assign, schedule, monitor, review and update training programmes remotely and systematically.

  • Cloud-based, remote access
  • Define training needs and deliver expected results
  • Lowest possible cost

Display & Motion

A seamless three-screen display gives an immersive training view of up to 180° over a custom airside terrain with international-standard markings, signage and lighting.

  • Seamless immersive view of up to 180°
  • Custom airside terrain, generic or geo-specific
  • AI traffic of aircraft, vehicles and equipment
