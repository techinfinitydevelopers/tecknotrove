About Tecknotrove

Simulation and training technology, built in-house since 2002.

From our Mumbai headquarters we design the motion platforms, visuals, controls and instructor software that make up every Tecknotrove simulator.

Who we are

One company. Every subsystem.

Tecknotrove designs and builds full-motion simulators for defence, aviation, automobile and industrial operators. Because every subsystem is engineered in-house, each simulator ships as one integrated system instead of a stack of third-party vendors.

Our simulators are in service with customers across 35+ countries, and our team provides technical support 24×7.

  • ISO 9001:2015 certified
  • 1,500+ simulators delivered
  • Headquartered in Mumbai, India
